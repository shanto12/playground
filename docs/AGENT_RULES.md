# Rules for every agent on the Curry District project

**Mission.** Curry District (Indian restaurant, 11851 FM 423 Ste 200, Little Elm, TX 75068) is delicious but its web presence, Google Business photos and social look flat and dull. A freelance designer is pitching the owners a "Brand Glow-Up". We are producing: two alternative websites, a mobile pitch hub, and a large set of design samples (packaging, wallpaper/murals, menus, social, signage, motion). Everything is shown to the owners **on a phone**, so mobile-first quality is the bar. The people looking at this must say "wow, that's us — and way better."

## The two brand directions (see `brand/tokens.css` and `brand/BRAND.md`)
* **A · BAZAAR** — loud, joyful, saturated: marigold / saffron / rani pink / peacock teal on cream & deep indigo; Bowlby One + DM Sans + Caveat; truck-art, Jaipur block-print, marigold garlands, hard offset "sticker" shadows, thick indigo outlines.
* **B · ROYAL** — jewel-toned and premium but still colourful: midnight aubergine / emerald / ruby / peacock with saffron-gold foil; Fraunces + Hanken Grotesk; Mughal jali, arches, paisley, brass & foil details.
Both are colourful, appetising and "Texas-family-restaurant friendly". Both must feel original, polished and intentional — never generic templates.

## Hard rules
1. **Work only inside your assigned folder** (named in your task). Never edit another agent's folder. You may READ anything in `/home/user/playground`. Shared inputs (`brand/`, `data/`, `research/`) are read-only unless your task says otherwise.
2. **Do NOT run any git commands** and do not deploy to Netlify — the orchestrator does that.
3. **Self-contained static output.** Plain HTML/CSS/vanilla JS/SVG/PNG/MP4. No build step, no external CDNs, no hot-linked images. Use the self-hosted fonts (`brand/fonts/fonts.css` + woff2 files — copy what you need into your own folder and fix relative paths). If you want a library (e.g. GSAP), `npm pack`/`npm i` it into your folder and vendor the min.js; the npm registry is reachable, most other hosts are NOT (the sandbox egress proxy blocks them — don't waste time on curl to random sites; WebSearch works for research).
4. **No third-party logos, trademarks, stock photos or copyrighted imagery.** Everything visual is original (SVG/CSS/canvas) or generated by us. You may *describe* big chains' ideas in text but never reproduce their marks or artwork. Do not copy Curry District's real logo/photos (we cannot see them anyway) — we design new concepts.
5. **Truthfulness.** Business facts come from `data/business.json` / `research/`. Never invent awards, review quotes, prices, health claims, or "halal/vegan/gluten-free" claims. Prices only where research marked them verified. If a fact is missing, leave it out or mark it clearly as a placeholder in `docs/OPEN_QUESTIONS.md` (append a line, don't overwrite).
6. **Concept disclaimer.** Every public page footer carries: *"Independent design concept prepared as a proposal — not the official Curry District website."* Every HTML page has `<meta name="robots" content="noindex,nofollow">`.
7. **Mobile-first & accessible.** Design at 390×844 first, then check 768 and 1280. No horizontal scroll. Tap targets ≥ 44px. Text contrast ≥ 4.5:1 (check it, especially cream-on-orange). Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`. Images need alt text; decorative SVG gets `aria-hidden="true"`. Real `<button>`/`<a>` elements. Keep each page's weight reasonable (< 2.5 MB incl. fonts where possible; lazy-load below-the-fold media).
8. **Visual QA loop is mandatory.** Chromium + Playwright are installed. Render what you build and LOOK at it (use the Read tool on the PNG — you can see images). Fix overlap, clipping, ugly spacing, off-brand colours, broken fonts, empty areas. Iterate at least twice. Do not report "done" on anything you haven't looked at.
   ```js
   const { chromium } = require('/opt/node-tools/node_modules/playwright');
   const b = await chromium.launch();               // default chromium works, no executablePath needed
   const p = await b.newPage({ viewport:{width:390,height:844}, deviceScaleFactor:2 });
   await p.goto('file:///home/user/playground/<your-folder>/index.html'); await p.waitForTimeout(800);
   await p.screenshot({ path:'<your-folder>/_qa/mobile.png', fullPage:true }); await b.close();
   ```
   The machine has only 4 CPUs / 16 GB shared by ~25 agents: use **one browser at a time, close it promptly**, never leave Chromium running, avoid huge fullPage captures at deviceScaleFactor 3, and don't loop screenshots in tight timers. Put QA screenshots in `<your-folder>/_qa/` (they will be stripped before deploy).
9. **Quality bar = agency work.** Strong visual hierarchy, generous spacing, consistent radius/shadow language, real typographic scale, delightful but purposeful motion (CSS/Web Animations/canvas/SVG-SMIL; 60fps; transform/opacity only). Copy is warm, witty, short, and specific to Indian food culture — no lorem ipsum, no "Welcome to our website".
10. **Cultural respect.** Celebrate South Asian design (block-print, rangoli, jali, paisley, marigolds, truck art, chai culture, Bollywood poster energy) without caricature: no turbans-and-elephants clichés, no deity imagery, no sacred symbols as decoration, no "exotic" language.
11. **Final reply format (≤ 200 words):** what you produced (file paths), what you verified by looking, known gaps/risks, and anything the orchestrator must wire up. No marketing fluff.
