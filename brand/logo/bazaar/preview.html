Bazaar
logo system

A steaming bowl whose curry rises like the sun through a cusped bazaar gate — the doorway into the District. Thick indigo outlines, flat festival colour, one hard sticker shadow, and DISTRICT set on a real street-sign plate. Every letter is outlined vector, so files render identically with no fonts installed.

Clear space & minimum size

X = the cap height of CURRY (the DISTRICT street-sign is almost exactly the same height, so either works as a ruler). Keep at least 1X clear on every side of any lockup. For the emblem alone, X = ¼ of the emblem's width; for the stamp, ⅛ of its diameter. Nothing else (text, photos, edges) goes inside that zone.
