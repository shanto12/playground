Brand Glow-Up · Direction B · Royal

The District Gate logo system

A Mughal pointed arch — the gateway into the neighbourhood’s flavour quarter — frames a brass handi breathing three ribbons of steam. Its cusped inner arch, pearl beading and the tiny arcade engraved round the pot are drawn like jali and brasswork, so the mark feels carved, not clip-art. “Curry” is set in Fraunces with hand-tuned kerning over a widely-spaced DISTRICT bracketed by the gate’s lamp-glint lozenges. Everything is outlined vector, built for saffron-gold foil on midnight, yet holds up as a one-colour stamp, a wax seal and a 24px favicon.
