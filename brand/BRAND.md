# Curry District — Brand Glow-Up: the two directions

> Positioning for both: **"The neighbourhood's flavour capital."** A "district" = a vibrant, walkable quarter of town with its own street life, signage, colour and characters. We lean into the *district / bazaar / quarter* idea: street signs, district maps, "Welcome to the District" stamps, zones/neighbourhoods on the menu (e.g. *Tandoor Quarter, Curry Quarter, Biryani Boulevard, Street-Food Lane, Sweet Street*).

## A · BAZAAR  (loud · joyful · family-and-friends energy)
* **Mood:** a festival street market at golden hour. Marigold garlands, painted trucks, block-print fabrics, Bollywood-poster typography, hot sticker shadows.
* **Palette:** ink indigo `#1D1147`, cream `#FFF4DC`, marigold `#FFB000`, saffron `#FF6A13`, rani pink `#E4147E`, chili `#D62839`, peacock teal `#00A8A0`, cilantro `#3FA34D`, electric violet `#7B5CFF` (tiny doses).
* **Type:** Bowlby One (display, uppercase, tight) · DM Sans (body) · Caveat (handwritten asides, price tags, arrows).
* **Shape language:** thick 3px indigo outlines, hard offset shadows (`6px 6px 0`), blobby stickers, scalloped/rangoli borders, arches, tilted cards (±2°), pill buttons.
* **Motion:** bouncy springs, marquee tickers, wobbling stickers, steam wisps, spice-dust particles, scroll-triggered pop-ins, tap ripples, confetti of marigold petals.
* **Tone of voice:** playful, loud-and-proud, food-first. "Butter chicken so good it needs its own zip code." Short punchy lines, a sprinkle of Hindi/Hinglish used lightly and correctly ("Chai-lo", "Swaad", "Bas, ek aur naan").
* **Best for:** younger families, delivery-app shoppers, Instagram/Reels, party-tray catering, lunch rush.

## B · ROYAL  (rich · premium · date-night & celebration)
* **Mood:** a modern palace dining room — jewel tones, brass, candlelight, jali screens and arches, saffron-gold foil on midnight.
* **Palette:** midnight aubergine `#160B26`, plum `#4B1D52`, emerald `#0F4D3F`, peacock `#117C86`, ruby `#A3173F`, saffron-gold `#E9A63A` (+ light `#F7D98A`, dark `#B7791F`), ivory `#FBF3E4`, blush `#F4C9BB`.
* **Type:** Fraunces (display; use 600/800 and italics for pull-quotes) · Hanken Grotesk (body). Gold-foil gradient for key words and hairline frames.
* **Shape language:** arches (mihrab), jali/lattice patterns at low opacity, hairline gold borders, soft glows, generous whitespace, large rounded cards (28px), slim pill buttons.
* **Motion:** slow elegant reveals, gold shimmer sweep across foil text, parallax on lattices, floating embers/diyas, steam, gentle Ken-Burns on dish art, smooth section transitions.
* **Tone of voice:** warm, confident, sensory. "Slow-simmered. Tandoor-fired. Made for sharing." Sentences that smell like cardamom.
* **Best for:** date nights, family celebrations, catering & events, upscale-casual perception, higher average ticket.

## Shared system
* **Wordmark:** "Curry District" — Bazaar: stacked, chunky, with a steaming-bowl sun emblem; Royal: refined serif lockup with an arch-and-bowl monogram "CD". Always provide: primary, horizontal, stacked, emblem-only, mono/one-colour, reversed.
* **Spice scale:** 5-step chili icon set (Mild · Medium · Hot · Extra-Hot · "District Hot").
* **Diet marks:** veg (green square), non-veg (red/brown square) — the Indian standard — plus text labels.
* **Menu zones ("districts"):** Starters Square · Tandoor Quarter · Curry Quarter · Biryani Boulevard · Indo-Chinese Alley · Bread Bazaar · Sweet Street · Chai & Coolers.
* **Photography direction (for the real shoot we will propose):** overhead & 45° shots on coloured/terrazzo/brass surfaces, steam caught in backlight, hands tearing naan, saturated but natural colour, one hero dish per frame, props: marigolds, brass katoris, banana leaf, cut limes.
