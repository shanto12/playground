11

Curtain & arch wipe

Bazaar draws a bright curtain and Royal raises a mihrab arch. It works between sections and between pages on the same site. Plain links behave normally, and new-tab or modified clicks are never intercepted.

Init & destroy

It auto-starts on DOMContentLoaded. Motion.destroy() removes every listener, observer, canvas and injected node and restores the original markup. Motion.init() brings it all back and also wires up any markup added since.

Kit running. 
<script src="motion.min.js" defer></script>           // auto-init
<script src="motion.min.js" defer data-manual></script> // then Motion.init()
