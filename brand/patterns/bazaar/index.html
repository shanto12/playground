On the wall

Our two favourites at room scale: Jaipur Arches for a bright daytime feature wall, Marigold Garland (night) for the evening dining room.

Using the tiles

  • Web: background: url(07-jaipur-arches-sunset.svg) 0 0 / 240px repeat; — keep the size a whole number of pixels so the repeat stays crisp.
  • Print: the SVGs are vector and flat-colour (no gradients or transparency), so any repeat size works. Suggested repeat: 45–60 cm for wallpaper, 12–18 cm for tissue & wraps, 6–8 cm for napkins.
  • Files: 240- or 320-unit square tiles, all under 30 KB, colours taken straight from brand/tokens.css. PNG previews (1024 px, 4×4 repeats) live in png/.
