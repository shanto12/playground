A menu that reads like a map

Curry District cooks Andhra fire, tandoor smoke, dum biryani and wok-tossed Indo-Chinese, all on one menu. We turned that big, generous list into eight easy-to-read zones, with clear icons and a print kit that looks like you.

02

The system: eight streets, one read

Every printed piece, board and screen uses the same six rules.

  1. Zones, not listsStarters Square, Tandoor Quarter, Curry Quarter, Biryani Boulevard, Indo-Chinese Alley, Bread Bazaar, Sweet Street, Chai & Coolers. Each one has a number, a colour and a tagline.
  2. Clear hierarchyThe dish name comes first, then the icons, then one short line. Curries are grouped by vegetarian, chicken, goat and lamb.
  3. Icons with wordsVeg and non-veg squares, plus 1–4 chillies (Royal counts in gems). One legend explains them, so there’s no colour-only code.
  4. Names you can scanNames in Title Case, with no item codes and no walls of capitals. Portions (“6 pcs”) are quiet notes.
  5. Dish art as signpostsOriginal illustrations mark each zone, in frames sized so real photos can drop in later.
  6. Answers in printThe allergen note and “ask us to make it milder or hotter” sit on every piece. The Guest favorite badge appears on 8 dishes only.

Built to fit: an auto-fit script checks every column. When space runs short it keeps descriptions for favorites and unfamiliar dishes (Kodi Vepudu, Gongura, Rajugaari) and lets well-known classics stand on their names. Type never drops below 9 pt for dish names.

Paper & finish suggestions (indicative)
  • Dine-in menu: matte 100# cover, printed both sides. For everyday wipe-downs, add matte lamination or choose a washable synthetic stock.
  • Table tent: 14–16 pt matte card, scored to fold. A washable synthetic version lasts longer.
  • Door-hanger: 14 pt cover with the die-cut shown in magenta on the crop-mark proof.
  • Wall boards: 16:9 stills for digital screens, or as film inserts for light boxes.

Confirm stock, colour (CMYK proof) and quantities with your printer. Prices and dishes get synced with the live menu before anything goes to press.
