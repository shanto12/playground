    Why start with the room? In the public reviews we could read, two mention a basic dining area and two mention noisy TVs on different channels. That is a very small sample, so take it as a hint, not a verdict. Muting the TVs or giving one corner a calm audio zone costs nothing, and the wall does the rest. It is a concept, not a commitment to renovate.

    08

    Your first weekend

    The $500 plan as three small days. About $490 in all, and no installer needed.

    1. Friday evening

      Close the loop

      • Get a yes from the landlord for the wall you chose
      • Hold a physical swatch against it
      • Buy the paint, bulbs and garlands

    2. Saturday

      Paint one wall or nook

      • Washable paint, about $120
      • Satin or washable matte, not flat
      • Label a can for touch-ups

    3. Sunday

      Dress it

      • 10–12 faux marigold garlands, about $120
      • 2700K, CRI 90+ bulbs, about $80
      • Chalkboard A-frame, about $110 (check the 12 sq ft cap)
      • QR cards on the tables, about $60

    Prices are budgeting ranges. The A-frame is sourced; paint, garlands, bulbs and cards are estimates that still need a quote.
