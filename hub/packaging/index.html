Who makes it

Online restaurant-supply stores

Stock containers by the case, with a one-case minimum V. A few listings are tagged customisable, but programme minimums and pricing were not retrieved.

Custom bag and label printers

Printed handled bags start around 250 pieces V. Small runs of roll labels cost more per label V, so order stickers at 1k and up.

Local DFW printers

Not researched yet. Ask for water-based flexo on kraft, and a food-contact statement for any print that touches food E.

Overseas factories

Minimums of 5,000–50,000, about 4–8 weeks by sea, and tariff exposure that is uncertain E. Ask for test reports, a pre-production sample and the dieline PDF.

Walk-in restaurant-supply stores

Plain stock for a fast start E. Pair it with custom stickers, stamps or sleeves so the brand still shows.

Print-on-demand

Good for staff tees, aprons, totes, mugs and stickers. Not for food-contact packaging E.

Confirm with supplier templates. Our dielines are indicative, drawn for concepting. Always request the converter’s own dieline PDF and a proof before anything is printed.

Print stays on the outside. Artwork goes on outer faces, stickers and bags. We avoid printing the food side of lids and trays unless the printer supplies a food-contact declaration for the ink, coating and adhesive.

Words we leave off the mockups. No “compostable”, “eco”, “halal” or “vegan” unless you confirm it and it is certified or true. On foam: we found no statewide foam ban in Texas and no Little Elm foam or packaging ordinance. That means “not found”, so verify with the Town before finalising.

06

Your first 30 days

Start with the cheapest pieces that show up in every delivery photo, then work up to the big print runs.

  1. Days 1–7

    Stickers & stuffer card

    • Pick a direction and lock the colour and pattern.
    • Proof the 2 in seal set and the stuffer card with a QR.
    • Small first run, so the new look is in the next delivery bag.

  2. Days 8–14

    Bags

    • Request supplier dielines and a sample for the 10 × 5 × 13 in handled bag.
    • Printed bags start around 250 pieces V.
    • Napkin proof goes out at the same time.

  3. Days 15–24

    Containers

    • Plain stock plus wrap labels and belly bands on pails, tubs and clamshells.
    • Test with real food: heat, steam and leaks, before a bulk order.
    • Sauce packets join once the cast is approved.

  4. Days 25–30

    Party trays & cups

    • Half-pan lid labels and carton labels, only if you cater.
    • Chai sleeves and lassi cups.
    • Review what moved and plan the next print run.

Thirty days assumes domestic printing. Supplier lead times vary, and overseas factories can take 4–8 weeks by sea E.
