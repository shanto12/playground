Festive, never careless

Celebrate, don’t explain

Greetings and food, nothing more. No deity imagery or sacred symbols: we use string lights, marigolds, kites, color and rangoli-style geometry. Eid and Ganesh Chaturthi stay text-led, and community members review them before anything is published.

Dates are verified yearly

Diwali, Holi, Eid, Ugadi and the rest follow lunar calendars. Every date carries an “approx” flag until someone checks it. Google special hours go in a month ahead.

No offers we can’t stand behind

No prices, discounts, halal or “authentic” claims, no flags on food packaging and no league or team trademarks (“the big game”, “match night”). Any offer needs the owners’ sign-off first.
