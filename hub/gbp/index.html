Colour direction

Let the food be the hero and let colour come from props and surfaces: marigold, peacock teal, ruby and rani pink cloths, indigo boards, brass and steel katoris. Rich colour that still looks real. One hero dish per frame.

A · Bazaar
B · Royal

Light & camera

  • Daylight or 5,200–5,600 K. Lock the white balance; no mixed lighting.
  • Key light back-left at 10 o’clock for steam and texture, white bounce front-right.
  • Overheads: soft top light. Exteriors: golden hour and blue hour.
  • Shoot 4:5, 1:1 and 9:16-safe; keep the subject centred.
  • Add 10–15 s vertical clips: steam pull, naan tear, sizzle.

Avoid

  • Orange or green colour casts from room lights
  • Harsh on-camera flash, heavy filters or presets
  • Stock images, fake props, food that has sat too long
  • Clutter: one hero dish per frame
  • Sacred symbols or deity imagery as decoration
  • Faces without a signed release; other brands’ logos
