Motion & reels

Movement is what makes people stop scrolling

Your food already moves: steam lifting off a karahi, a lid coming off the biryani, gold catching the light. Here that movement is put to work as reels, story ads, a logo sting, TV menu boards and website touches, ready to post.

A · Bazaar B · Royal

01

Why it works

A feed is a thumb moving fast. Three things slow it down.

The first second and a half

Steam rising, a lid lifting, foil catching light. A small, real movement up front earns the next ten seconds of attention.

Loops get a second look

Our story ads and menu boards loop with no visible seam, so a six-second piece can play twice before anyone swipes away.

Made to work on mute

Lots of people scroll with the sound off. Every piece tells its story in pictures and a few words on screen, and sound is a bonus when it plays.

    06

    Production notes

    The practical bits: files, sound, timing and accessibility.

    Formats & files
    • Video: MP4 (H.264, yuv420p), 30 fps, “fast start” so it begins playing before it finishes downloading. Each file is under 3 MB.
    • Sizes: 1080×1920 for reels and stories, 1920×1080 for TV boards and website heroes, 1080×1080 for the square sting and ornaments.
    • Safe zones: on reels, key words stay out of the top 150 px and bottom 250 px. Stories leave room for the reply bar too.
    • With every video: a full-size poster JPG, a small thumbnail and an 8-frame storyboard.
    • Silent masters: files ship without sound, so you can add sound in the app or leave them muted on screens.
    Timing & sound

    Every cut is timed to a beat, so a track at the tempo below will feel “locked”. Suggestions only. Use original sound or a properly licensed track.

    The Gateway · 15 s100 BPMA quiet drone and room tone to open. A soft brass shimmer as the logo draws in (1.8 s). An air “whoosh” through the arch (3.9 s). One light hand-drum tap each time a dish lands (every 1.2 s, two beats). A rising swell under the ember rise (11 s), then one warm low note on the end card (12.2 s).
    Logo sting · 4 sfree timeLow singing-bowl swell as the arch draws, a single plucked string as the handi lands (1.0 s), a chime on the gold sweep (2.0 s), let it ring out.
    Story ads · 6 s loops80 BPMTwo bars loop perfectly in 6 seconds. A simmer or sizzle bed, a cup clink or ladle tap on beat 1 of bar 2. Keep it under the voice of the room.
    Menu boards · 8 s loopssilent / 60 BPMPlay muted in the dining room. Online, a soft 60 BPM pad gives eight beats per loop.
    Ornaments · 4 s loops60 or 120 BPMFour (or eight) beats per loop. Use as quiet background texture under a voice-over or text post.
    Music & licensing
    • Business accounts get a smaller music library than personal ones, and popular songs and film music are usually off-limits for brands.
    • Your best sound is free: record 10 seconds of the tandoor, a sizzling karahi, a pour of chai. Original audio also tends to get reused by others.
    • If you add music, use a royalty-free library with a commercial licence and keep the licence on file.
    • No copyrighted tracks are used or implied in anything here.
    Accessibility
    • No flashing: nothing flashes more than three times a second. Every change is a slow fade, sweep or drift.
    • Captions: every word on screen is also in the post caption, and posts get alt text that describes the dish.
    • Contrast: ivory and gold on midnight, well above 4.5:1. Words never sit over busy art.
    • Reduced motion: on the websites, anyone who asks their phone for less motion sees a still poster instead.
    How these were made

    Each video is an animated illustration that is rendered one frame at a time, 30 frames a second, from the same artwork as the websites. Change a dish, a word or a colour and the whole set can be re-rendered in minutes. No stock footage, no hired music, nothing borrowed.

    When the real photo shoot happens, the same layouts take real dish photos and video in place of the illustrations.
