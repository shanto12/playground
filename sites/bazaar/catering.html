Good questions

Party FAQ

Straight answers, and where we’re not sure, we say so.

Do you cater parties?

We do: pre-booked platters for parties and events, plus outdoor catering. Call us to talk menu, headcount and timing.

Please call to confirm

How far ahead should I ask?

Catering is planned in advance, so the sooner the better. Please call to confirm lead times for your date.

Please call to confirm

Any vegetarian options?

Plenty. Paneer curries, dal, veg biryani, Gobi Manchurian, Chilli Paneer and more. The menu runs vegetarian and non-vegetarian, side by side.

Can you keep the heat down for the kids?

Heat levels are planned with you, from easy-going to full Andhra fire. Heads-up: some guests say even Mild has a kick, so tell us who’s eating.

What does catering cost?

It depends on the dishes and the headcount, so we price it when we plan it with you. Please call to confirm.

Is your food halal?

Please call us to confirm. It’s an important question, and we’d rather answer it directly than guess on a website.
