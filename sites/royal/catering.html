Chapter II

How it works

Three unhurried steps, from the first hello to the last tray.

  1. I

    Tell us the occasion

    Send the inquiry below or call us with your date, a rough guest count and the kind of gathering you have in mind.

  2. II

    Shape the menu together

    One personal conversation to balance vegetarian and non-vegetarian dishes and set spice levels, from mild to Andhra-style fire.

  3. III

    Pick up, or let us cater

    Collect pre-booked platters from our kitchen on FM 423, or ask about outdoor catering for larger occasions.

Catering is arranged in advance. Please call us to discuss your date and menu.

Chapter V

Questions, answered

And if yours is not here, we would love to hear it on the phone.

Can you cater our event?

With pleasure. We prepare pre-booked platters and offer outdoor catering for celebrations and gatherings. Call us to plan the menu and the details.

Is there a good choice for vegetarians?

A generous one: paneer curries, dal, vegetable biryani, Gobi Manchurian, Chilli Paneer and more. Our menu offers vegetarian and non-vegetarian dishes side by side.

How spicy is the food?

Spice levels are set with you, from mild to Andhra-style fire. Some guests find that even Mild carries a little warmth, so tell us about your crowd.

Is your food halal?

Please call us to confirm. It matters, and we would like to answer you personally rather than guess here.

Please call to confirm

Can you seat a large party?

Please call us to confirm before you arrive, so we can plan properly for your group.

Do you offer takeout and delivery?

Yes. Order online for pickup directly from us, or choose delivery through Uber Eats, DoorDash or Grubhub. Dining in is relaxed and counter-service.
