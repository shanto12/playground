## 1. What suits which food

| Food | Best pack | Why / watch-outs |
|---|---|---|
| Curries, dal, gravies | 16 or 32 oz PP deli tub + snap lid; PE-lined paper pail (26 oz) for lighter gravies | PP #5 is microwave-safe and leak-resistant. Oily gravy can weep through a pail's folded bottom [E]. Remove the pail's wire handle before microwaving [E]. |
| Biryani, pulao, rice | 2- or 3-compartment clamshell (PP or plant fiber), or 32 oz tub | Compartments keep raita and gravy off the rice. Leave a vent gap for steam [E]. |
| Tandoori, kababs, samosa, pakora, naan | Vented or corrugated kraft clamshell, kraft boat, paper bag, foil sleeve | A sealed PP lid steams crispy food soft. Line with parchment and vent the lid [E]. A corrugated kraft clamshell exists at 8x6, 190/case [V, Sabert]. |
| Chutneys, raita, sauces | 1–2 oz PP portion cups with lids | PP, not PET, for hot sauces [E]. |
| Desserts (gulab jamun, kheer) | 4–8 oz PP tubs (hot) or PET cups with domes (cold) | Syrup needs leak-proof lids [E]. |
| Lassi (cold) | 12–16 oz PET cup, dome or flat lid, straw | PET is not for hot liquids [E]. |
| Chai (hot) | 8–12 oz paper hot cup + lid + sleeve | Sleeve = free billboard. |
| Catering | Foil half-pans + lids, label on lid | Foil is not microwave-safe. Half-pan lid is $0.245 each [V, WebstaurantStore, $24.49/100]. |
| Add-ons | Bag-seal stickers, paper bags with handles, napkins, cutlery kit, bag-stuffer card, branded tape | Costs for cutlery kits and tape were not researched. |

**Material notes.** Plant-fiber/bagasse is a heat- and oil-tolerant compostable option. EcoChoice 9x6x3 two-compartment is $0.19–0.21 each at 200/case [V]. PLA-lined paper and PLA lids soften with hot, oily food [E]. Foam is a poor fit for hot oily curry anyway.

## 2. Food-contact, print and local rules (US/Texas)

- **No "FDA-approved ink" list exists.** Inks and coatings are treated as indirect food additives under 21 CFR 170–178 [V: [food-safety.com](https://www.food-safety.com/articles/7001-the-use-of-printing-inks-for-food-packaging-in-the-united-states), [Nazdar FDA letter](https://www.nazdar.com/Portals/0/Letters/FDA.pdf)].
- **Print on the outside surface** is generally fine when a functional barrier keeps ink from reaching food. Foil is a recognised barrier. Paperboard and thin film need migration testing, not assumptions [V: [Packaura](https://packaura.com/food-safe-printing-inks-fda-regulations/)].
- **Design implication.** Keep artwork on outer faces, stickers and bags. Avoid printing the food-side of lids and trays unless the converter supplies a food-contact declaration of compliance for ink, coating and adhesive. Ask for low-migration inks, since ink can "set off" onto the food side when stacked or rolled [E].
- **Common citations** are 21 CFR 175.300 (coatings) and 176.170 (paper/paperboard) [E]. See [UL Prospector](https://www.ulprospector.com/knowledge/13387/fundamentals-of-fda-food-contact-for-coatings-and-adhesives/).
- **PFAS** (grease-proofing) is a concern for bagasse/paper imports. Ask suppliers for a written PFAS-free statement [E, unverified].
- **FTC Green Guides** (general knowledge, [E]): do not print "compostable", "biodegradable" or "eco" unless the product is certified (e.g. BPI) and local composting exists. DFW composting access: not found.
- **Texas foam ban: none found.** Tex. Health & Safety Code §361.0961(a) bars local governments from restricting, for solid-waste purposes, the sale or use of a container or package in a manner not authorized by state law [V: [Justia 2019 text](https://law.justia.com/codes/texas/2019/health-and-safety-code/title-5/subtitle-b/chapter-361/subchapter-c/section-361-0961/); [Austin Common](https://theaustincommon.com/could-texas-be-getting-a-styrofoam-ban/); [PlasticBagLaws](https://www.plasticbaglaws.org/preemption)]. One result claimed Houston bans foam and another listed Texas as having a ban. These conflict with the preemption sources and are not relied on. A federal "Farewell to Foam Act" is proposed, not law, as of those reports [[Packaging Dive](https://www.packagingdive.com/news/farewell-to-foam-act-congress/702218/)].
- **Little Elm: no foam or packaging ordinance found.** Search surfaced only Ordinance 1536 (Chapter 46, grease traps) and the [Food Permits page](https://www.littleelm.gov/1303/Food-Permits). Confirm with the Town before finalising.
- **Delivery tamper-evidence in Texas: not found.**

- **Printful / Printify** [E]: print-on-demand merch (staff tees, aprons, totes, mugs, stickers). Not for food-contact packaging.
- **Local DFW printers**: not researched. Ask for water-based flexo on kraft, plus a food-contact statement for any inside print.
- **Alibaba cautions** [E]: MOQs of 5,000–50,000, 4–8 weeks by sea, and tariff exposure that is uncertain (verify). "FDA registered" is not "FDA approved". Demand test reports (migration, heavy metals, PFAS), a pre-production sample and the dieline PDF. The importer carries compliance risk.
- **Restaurant-supply stores** (Restaurant Depot and similar) [E]: stock plain packaging for fast starts. Pair plain stock with custom stickers, stamps or sleeves.

## 4. What makes packaging Instagram-shareable [E]

- A **sticker seal** that doubles as a mini poster (2 in circle, strong motif, one witty line).
- A **hidden message** the customer finds on opening. Put it on a bag-stuffer card or the bag's inner panel rather than the food side of the lid (see section 2).
- A **bottom-of-box surprise** ("you've reached the best part").
- **Sauce cups** as a collectible illustrated cast (mood or spice level), kept respectful (rule 10).
- **Photo-friendly colour** that stays on-brand under a phone flash.
- A **QR** to the Clover online ordering page or a Google review page [links in `data/business.json`, unverified]. Google's review policy forbids incentivised reviews [E]. Do not invent a loyalty program or offer.

## 5. Delivery-app specifics [E, unverified]

- **Bag seal.** A branded tamper-evident sticker across the bag fold is the highest-leverage item. It protects the order and puts the brand in the customer's first photo.
- **Thank-you card or stuffer.** A QR to first-party ordering is useful because marketplaces usually keep customer contact data. Check each app's terms about off-platform promotion.
- **Menu photos.** Upload in the Uber Eats Manager and DoorDash Merchant Portal item editors. Typical rules are the real dish, good light and no text, logo or watermark. Confirm current specs in each portal.
- **Mockups are not app photos.** Real photography of the real dish, plated or in branded packaging, is needed.
