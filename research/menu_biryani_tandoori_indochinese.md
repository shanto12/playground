## Limits
- Every aggregator, delivery-app and official-site URL was blocked by the egress proxy, and the session WebSearch budget (200/200) ran out after 8 successful searches.
- Data comes from search-result snippets only. The search tool did not say which page each price came from, so `sources` lists the pages the search returned, not confirmed per-price citations.
