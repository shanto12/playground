# Indian / South-Asian Brand Leaders: Design Lessons for Curry District

Prepared 2026-10-04. **Method and limits:** 16 WebSearch queries returned results, then the session's search budget (200) was hit. WebFetch was blocked for every host tried, so findings come from search-result summaries, not full pages.
**Confidence labels:** **H** = stated in a search result with a named source. **M** = secondary or inferred from a summary. **L** = my background knowledge, not verified this session.
Brands with no verified detail are listed in "Not verified" at the end. Nothing here is a Curry District business fact.

## 1. Case notes

**Dishoom (London) - the benchmark.**
- **Agencies:** & SMITH (identity, logo, menus, signage, cocktail packaging) and Afroditi Krassa, later Russell Sage (interiors) [H]. Sources: [Logo Designer](http://www.logo-designer.co/smith-creates-vintage-bombay-branding-identity-for-dishoom-restaurant-kings-cross/), [Afroditi](https://www.afroditi.com/dishoom), [Macaulay Sinclair](https://macaulaysinclair.com/case-study/dishoom-carnaby/). They brought in Indian-typeface expert Kalapi Gajjar-Bordawekar so the lettering felt authentic [H].
- **Story:** not "India" but one specific, fading institution, the Bombay Irani café. Look and feel mix vintage Bombay with old Indian train ephemera [H]. Each café has its own backstory; King's Cross is a "Godown" railway warehouse [H]. Source: [Design Week](https://www.designweek.co.uk/issues/november-2014/smith-brings-1920s-bombay-to-dishoom-kings-cross/).
- **Palette and interior:** an almost monochrome interior of checkerboard tiles, oak panelling, Carrara marble tables, mismatched chairs, ornate painted mirrors and old clocks, in dim light [H]. Carnaby adds a jukebox rescued from Chor Bazaar and Bombay Progressives art prints [H]. Source: [Wallpaper](https://www.wallpaper.com/travel/uk/london/restaurants/dishoom-carnaby). Colour comes from food, drinks and ephemera, not walls.
- **Type:** a serif/grotesque mix (Gill Sans, Cheltenham, Berthold Akzidenz-Grotesk), with a different masthead face per site: Gill Sans, Clearface and Sim Hei [H]. Source: [Design Week](https://www.designweek.co.uk/issues/november-2014/smith-brings-1920s-bombay-to-dishoom-kings-cross/).
- **Photographed because:** the bacon naan roll, an ownable hero dish [H, [Time Out](https://www.timeout.com/london/restaurants/hype-dish-dishooms-cult-bacon-naan-roll)], plus the checkerboard and marble set. Dishoom later sold a DIY bacon-naan kit [H, [Time Out](https://www.timeout.com/london/news/dishoom-has-launched-a-diy-kit-for-its-famous-bacon-naan-roll-061720)].
- **Lesson:** a niche, true story and restraint beat decoration. Its social look is a gap I could not verify.

**Curry Up Now (San Francisco) - the closest fast-casual match.**
- **Agency:** Design Womb (launched 2010, refreshed for franchising) [H]. Sources: [Design Womb](https://designwomb.com/work/curry-up-now), [Behance](https://www.behance.net/gallery/1439813/Curry-Up-Now-Branding-Restaurant-Food-Truck-Design).
- **Look:** a "curry-inspired" palette of vibrant, arresting pairings, a hand-drawn logo, and type inspired by Indian street-food carts [H]. Indian patterns get "playful twists", with branded menu iconography and a full packaging system [H].
- **Voice:** the menu puns ("Naughty Naan", tikka masala burrito) are the brand [H, [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Curry_Up_Now)]. The food truck, restaurant and box share one language.
- **Lesson:** this is Direction A's proof point. Bright and playful scales to franchising when patterns, icons and packaging come from one kit.

**Chai Pani (Asheville, Atlanta, DC) - the closest match for BAZAAR.**
- **Interior:** marigolds dripping from the foyer ceiling, pink, turquoise and yellow walls, and two large wooden dancing tigers [H]. The tigers came from an ad for a wrestling pit in the owner's hometown [H]. About 15 artists painted murals and hand-lettered signs imitating Indian typography [H]. Old Hindi film posters hang on the walls [H]. Sources: [Garden & Gun](https://gardenandgun.com/first-look-chai-pani-finds-its-new-asheville-home), [Mountain Xpress](https://mountainx.com/food/small-bites-chai-pani-reopens-with-a-fresh-look/), [Chai Pani Restaurant Group](https://www.chaipanirestaurantgroup.com/about).
- **People:** Molly Irani leads space design; brand director Michael Files had lived in Mumbai [H]. James Beard Outstanding Restaurant, 2022 [H].
- **Photographed because:** the marigold canopy and mural walls.
- **Lesson:** commission real artists and use real signage lettering. A personal story (the tiger) reads as authentic, where a generic animal motif would not.

**Rasa (DC) - fast-casual modernist.**
- **Agency:** REQ (strategy, identity, creative) [H, [REQ](https://req.co/case-studies/rasa/)].
- **Look:** a custom logo typeface in vibrant colours, with airy interiors, modern artwork, a bright green door and bronze pendants [H, [WTOP](https://wtop.com/food-restaurant/2024/05/gateway-to-culture-how-rasa-owners-are-using-their-experiences-to-make-indian-food-more-accessible-2/)]. The positioning is "flavourful, healthy food that just happens to be Indian" [H], so it deliberately avoids heritage kitsch.
- **Caution:** a Yelp listing shows one DC location as closed [M, [Yelp](https://www.yelp.com/biz/rasa-washington-2)]. Design alone does not carry a restaurant.
- **Lesson:** a custom wordmark and a bowl-first, health-forward story work for a younger audience, but this is the least "Indian-looking" example. BAZAAR should keep more culture than this.

**Indienne (Chicago) - the ROYAL-adjacent modern take.**
- **Look:** cream and green with brass accents, pastel pink banquettes, a Calacatta marble bar with antique mirrors, and a Holi painting by Ken Andjulis, in a 19th-century printing warehouse with exposed brick and beams [H]. Sources: [Hospitality Design](https://hospitalitydesign.com/news/f-b/indienne-chicago/), [Chicago Magazine](https://www.chicagomag.com/dining-drinking/indienne-is-precise-and-articulated-fine-dining/).
- **Lesson:** premium Indian can be soft and unexpected. Pastel pink and green beat the expected maroon and gold, and a single festival artwork carries the culture.

**Pondicheri (Houston) - the Texas precedent.**
- **Look:** a sleek steel-and-glass shell with handcrafted doors. Tiles from a New Delhi artisan, patterned upholstery from open-air markets, and sari fabric add colour [H]. Source: [Houston Chronicle](https://www.houstonchronicle.com/entertainment/top-100-restaurants/article/Pondicheri-Cafe-6508925.php).
- **Lesson:** a neutral modern container with a few genuinely crafted Indian objects works, and a Texas diner has already accepted it. The Bake Lab bakery upstairs shows that retail and dessert extend the brand [H].

**Behrouz Biryani (India) - the packaging benchmark.**
- **Constraint:** a black carton and a single print colour [H]. Royal cues were distilled to simple forms, with sparing ornament and negative space doing the work, so the pack reads premium from delivery-bag distance [H]. Source: [portfolio page](https://www.harividyanand.com/behrouzbiryanipackaging).
- **Positioning:** the story moved from a "secret recipe from a 2,000-year-old Persian kingdom" to "the Royal Biryani that makes a moment special" [H, [afaqs](https://www.afaqs.com/news/advertising/rebel-foods-behrouz-biryani-moves-on-from-secret-recipe-narrative-to-making-moments-special)]. Tilt Brand Solutions is named on the repositioning [M]. BBH is named on a food campaign [M, [exchange4media](https://www.exchange4media.com/advertising-news/behrouz-biryanibbh-team-up-to-create-visually-appealing-food-campaign-90448.html)].
- **Lesson:** this is Direction B's packaging rule. One foil or ink colour on a dark box looks more luxurious than six colours.

**Dallas-Fort Worth reference points.**
- **Gymkhana Dallas:** royal blue banquettes, chandeliers, antiques from India, and hand-painted murals of regal figures, elephants and patterns, in a social-club concept [H, [CultureMap](https://dallas.culturemap.com/news/restaurants-bars/gymkhana-indian-downtown/)]. It is well executed but leans on the elephant and regal-figure clichés, so treat it as the look to differentiate from.
- **Tikka Shack:** born in Dallas, a spinoff of Masala Wok and pitched as "the Chipotle of Indian food" [H]. NICE branded it, with a pun name, guidelines for photography and fonts, menus and table toppers, and a "This Is Naan Nation" hashtag with staff apparel [H]. Sources: [CultureMap](https://dallas.culturemap.com/news/restaurants-bars/fast-casual-indian-tikka-shack/), [NICE](https://nice-branding.com/work/tikka-shack/). This is the direct local fast-casual competitor.
- **Others:** Roti Grill (The Star, Frisco) is sleek and order-at-counter. Nirvana Lounge is neon and art installations. The Yellow Chilli (Plano) is a casual fine-dining cocktail room [H, search summaries]. Little Elm's immediate competitors were not checked.

## 2. Recurring winning patterns

1. **Specific heritage with a twist.** Dishoom picks the Irani café, Chai Pani picks Mumbai chaat and street-food culture, and Behrouz picks Persian royalty. None says just "India" [H].
2. **A hero item or ritual to photograph.** Examples are the bacon naan, the marigold canopy, the jukebox and the tigers [H]. Curry District needs one.
3. **A pun-driven menu voice** (Naughty Naan, Naan Nation) that carries from sign to staff shirt to hashtag [H].
4. **Real artists and real lettering.** Chai Pani used about 15 artists, Indienne a local painter, and Dishoom a Devanagari type specialist [H].
5. **Constraint-led packaging.** Behrouz used one colour on black. Curry Up Now used a repeatable pattern and icon kit [H].
6. **Pattern as a system.** The pattern is reinterpreted (Curry Up Now) or limited to a few tiles and fabrics (Pondicheri), never wallpaper everywhere [H].
7. **Chai culture as an anchor.** The Chai Pani name, Dishoom's chai-and-café identity, and Bombay Sandwich Co's "Best Chai" press all point here [M].
8. **Fast casual goes loud and premium goes restrained.** Curry Up Now and Chai Pani are saturated. Indienne, Behrouz and Dishoom are tonal and use a few accents [H].
9. **Dark modern base plus craft.** Indienne's marble and brass, Pondicheri's steel and glass and Dishoom's marble and oak give colour a quiet stage [H].

## 3. Avoid (cliché audit)

Elephants and regal-figure murals (Gymkhana shows how common they are), turbans, the saffron-white-green flag palette, mandala wallpaper everywhere, Papyrus-style "exotic" faux-Devanagari fonts, and brown-and-beige "curry house" palettes. Also skip deity imagery, sacred symbols as decoration, the Taj Mahal silhouette and any "mystical India" copy. These are standard cliché-audit items [L], consistent with AGENT_RULES rule 10.

## Moodboard in words

**BAZAAR**
- A marigold ceiling you walk under at the door, with the pink, turquoise and yellow walls of a festival lane.
- A hand-painted "district street sign" and a sign-painter's shop-front lettering, made by a real local artist.
- A truck-art box that works as a lunch-rush logo from across a counter, with a pun on the lid ("Naan-stop").
- A chai cup with a sticker-bold shadow, a tilted price tag in marker script, and a steam swirl.
- One hero photographable item: a naan roll or "district burrito" with its own character mascot.

**ROYAL**
- A black or aubergine box with a single saffron-gold foil print and lots of empty space: Behrouz discipline.
- A single arch and jali screen, backlit, over a pastel-pink or emerald banquette.
- Marble, brass katoris and antique-style mirrors, with candlelight gleaming on the metal.
- Fraunces italic headlines in foil against deep plum, with one festival painting (rangoli or Holi) as the only mural.
- A tiny ritual: a brass tray of saunf and a hot-towel moment at the end, which people photograph.

## Do / Don't

**Do**
- Pick one specific story and one hero item, and make both ownable.
- Commission or draw real, hand-lettered signage.
- Keep pattern as a system (icons, borders, packaging), not wallpaper.
- Design packaging for the delivery-bag glance, one colour on a dark box for ROYAL and a sticker-bold box for BAZAAR.
- Use puns and Hinglish lightly and correctly.
- Let food, not decor, supply most of the colour in the premium direction.

**Don't**
- Don't use elephants, turbans, deity or sacred imagery.
- Don't use a tri-colour flag palette.
- Don't use mandala wallpaper, Papyrus-style faux-Indian fonts or brown-and-beige.
- Don't copy another brand's marks or artwork (only learn from their approach).
- Don't claim awards, reviews or dietary labels for Curry District that the research does not verify.
