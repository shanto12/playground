## 2. What customers love

Dishes, by number of summaries naming them (of 16):
1. **Biryani and pulav**, 7 mentions. Named items: Goat Biryani, Gongura Mutton Pulav, Chicken 65 pulav, Boneless Chicken Biryani, paneer and egg pulav. The **family-pack pulavs** are described as "consistently positive" for taste, authenticity, portion and value.
2. **Butter Chicken**, 6. Called creamy and the standout. Also the most-ordered item on DoorDash.
3. **Garlic naan**, 6. Described as "super tasty" and paired with the curries.
4. **Chicken Tikka Masala** (2), **chicken tikka kebab** (2, "very spicy"), **tandoori chicken** (1, "best in DFW" for some reviewers), **goat curry and chicken curry** (Yelp popular dishes).

Themes:
- **Flavour and authenticity (M).** Reviewers describe well-seasoned food and "a symphony of spices" with well-cooked rice (paraphrase).
- **Portions and value (M).** Portions are described as generous, and prices as reasonable for the portions. A $10–20 per-person band appeared once (L, unverified). Do not use it as a price.
- **Staff warmth (M, 5 summaries).** Staff are friendly and accommodating, and give good recommendations.
- **Consistency and spice honoured (M).** Praised for matching each customer's chosen heat level.
- **Clean, cozy room (M).** Background Indian music is mentioned. New tables and chairs were mentioned once (L).

**Quotes.** No review-level verbatims were visible. These fragments appeared in quotation marks inside summaries. The platform is unattributed, so they are not usable as testimonials until checked at the source:
- "Ya can't beat their Butter Chicken even if you tried!" (butter chicken summary)
- "very friendly and accommodating staff/management" (service summary)
- "the hospitable staff works hard, stays positive and makes this place wonderful" (service summary)
- "Perfect medium spicy" (spice summary)

## 4. Moments a brand can amplify

- **Weekday happy hour 4–7 PM** (M). The hours listing says weekdays; what is on offer is unknown.
- **Lunch** (M). Opens 11:00 and closes 2:30–3:00. A "lunch buffet" was hinted at once (L); confirm it exists.
- **Family dinners and family packs** (M). Pulav family packs. A weekend pack with a free appetizer and dessert appeared once (L, unverified).
- **Takeout and delivery** (M). It has the largest audience: DoorDash/Uber Eats 2,000+ ratings.
- **Catering** (L). Sulekha lists the business as a caterer.
- **Spice-your-way** (M). Three heat levels, with a reputation for honouring them.
- **Andhra and Hyderabadi biryani/pulav specialties** (M): Gongura, Chicken 65.
- **Gift cards** (L). A Giftly page exists.

## 5. Open flags
- Yelp's hours differ from `business.json` (Saturday open straight through to 10 PM; Sunday closes 9:30). Phones also differ. See Yelp and https://www.allmenus.com/tx/little-elm/815011-curry-district/menu/.
- Social handles say "Frisco" while the address is Little Elm.
- Yelp lists "vegan options". Make no vegan or halal claim in copy.

## Implications for design

1. **Ambience complaint (basic decor, noisy TVs) → a feature mural wall** behind the main seating, plus a photo-moment corner. Propose a "calm audio zone" and muted TVs as an option. Treat the mural as a concept, since there is no renovation commitment.
2. **Delivery is the biggest audience → branded, sealed, vented packaging.** No leak or cold complaints surfaced, so pitch it as protecting the 2,000+ ratings and not as a fix. Include a "rice included" checklist sticker, given the missing-rice report.
3. **Spice calibration → a visual heat scale** (Mild / Medium / Spicy, with honest copy such as "Mild still has a kick"). Put a chilli-pip icon on menus, bag stickers and lids.
4. **Hero dishes → lead with the proven favourites:** biryani/pulav, butter chicken, garlic naan, tikka and tandoori, in a "Fan Favourites" menu block with illustrations. No prices.
5. **Family packs → a large-format "Family Pack" box and weekend-table poster system** (verify the free appetizer and dessert offer first).
6. **Staff warmth → make hospitality part of the brand voice.** Add an apron/uniform concept and table talkers reading "Ask us what we'd order," since reviewers cite staff recommendations.
7. **Time-of-day moments → a social template pair:** "Lunch 11–2:30" and "Happy Hour 4–7" (the happy-hour offer is not yet specified).
8. **Review gap (Tripadvisor 0, Yelp lowest at 4.0) → a QR "tell us how we did" on bag stickers and table tents,** pointing to Google, Yelp and Tripadvisor.
9. **Proof, not invention → show only real, dated rating numbers** (Yelp 4.0, Google 4.2, Facebook 4.8, DoorDash 4.5), and write benefit lines as brand voice, never as fake quotes.
10. **Identity and tone → use "Little Elm" locally and unify handles.** Use the Andhra specialties (Gongura) as a storytelling pillar, in a warm, family-friendly register. Royal must stay approachable; Bazaar's energy fits the lively room.
