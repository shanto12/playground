## 2. Curry District's position

- **Ratings.** Google 4.2, Yelp 4.0 (120), Restaurantji 4.5 (479), Restaurant Guru 4.2 (1,021). Delivery apps show 4.5 with 2,000+ ratings (see `reviews_sentiment.md`, M).
- **Where it sits.** It is mid-pack on rating: below Kuppanna 4.7 and Delhi6 4.6, level with Spice Grove, above Hyderabad House. It has **solid volume but no visible brand**.
- **Breadth.** Rivals mostly specialise: South Indian (Kuppanna), pure veg (Veg Vihaar), biryani (Hyderabad House), fast-casual wraps (Blist'r, Roti Grill). CD is an all-rounder: curries, biryani and pulao, tandoor, Indo-Chinese. That gives it the **broadest family menu** in Little Elm.
- **Identity gap.** The social handles say "Frisco" while the address is Little Elm. A "West Frisco 4.4" snippet shows the name floating between towns.
- **Strengths to exploit.** Family packs, delivery volume, staff warmth and Andhra specialties. The happy hour (weekdays 4–7) is **unverified**.

## 3. White space (hypotheses; confirm visually)

1. **No bold, ownable colour identity** surfaced for any neighbourhood competitor. Only the concept brands (Blist'r, Roti Grill) read as designed.
2. **Packaging as a brand asset.** No snippet mentions branded bags, lids or stickers.
3. **Party-tray / catering branding.** Nobody is marketing named, branded "feast" trays, despite a large celebration culture.
4. **A "District" story.** Nobody uses neighbourhood or street-sign storytelling. CD's name allows it.
5. **Family and lake-day lifestyle.** Nobody ties to Lake Lewisville picnics, game-night packs or kids' menus.
6. **Premium-but-friendly.** RAASA and King are formal. A jewel-toned yet casual room is open.

## 4. Local context (from memory; **L**, verify on census.gov QuickFacts and the town sites)

- **Little Elm** grew from about 26k (2010) to roughly 46k (2020) and is still adding master-planned subdivisions. It skews young families with school-age children, with household income above the Texas median.
- **Frisco and Plano** hold a very large Indian-American population: roughly one in four residents in Frisco and more in parts of Plano. Both are 10–15 minutes from Little Elm.
- **Lake Lewisville** and the Little Elm lakefront drive summer foot traffic, July 4 and weekend park crowds.
- Frisco's Star and Legacy corporate campuses drive lunch catering demand.

## 5. Six local tie-ins

1. **Schools and PTAs** (Little Elm, Frisco, Prosper ISDs): spirit nights, teacher-appreciation tray boxes, a branded "school-night family pack".
2. **HOAs and neighbourhood groups** (Nextdoor, HOA socials, new-resident welcome cards): a pool-party tray and block-party catering menu. CD already won a Nextdoor "Neighborhood Favorite" (L; verify).
3. **Temples and community events** (e.g. Frisco and Plano temples): festival-day tray menus. Make **no vegetarian-only, vegan or halal claims** unless the kitchen confirms them.
4. **Diwali and Holi.** Diwali 2026 falls around **8 Nov (verify)**, about five weeks away: a sweets-box and thali pre-order drop. Holi is around March 2027.
5. **Cricket leagues.** Weekend leagues in Frisco, Plano and Lewisville parks, plus Major League Cricket at Grand Prairie (Texas Super Kings, ~35 mi; verify season). Sponsor team jerseys and offer a "match-day tray".
6. **Corporate lunch drops** in Frisco (The Star) and Legacy (about 10–15 mi): lunch boxes with a catering menu card and a pre-order QR, to test the delivery radius.

## 6. Online ordering across the set

Most rivals combine their own site, a Clover-type or direct ordering page, and 3P apps (DoorDash, Uber Eats, Grubhub, Postmates). CD already has Clover direct ordering (`business.json`). The design lever is **moving repeat customers from 3P apps to direct ordering** via bag inserts and QR cards.

## Implications for design

1. **Own the colour lane.** Bazaar is the strongest fit for a family Little Elm room, since no neighbour appears to own a loud identity. Royal should be pitched for catering, corporate and "date-night" use, where RAASA and King are the competition.
2. **Build a "District" system.** Street-sign badges, a neighbourhood map motif and a "Little Elm" wordmark fix the Frisco/Little Elm identity gap and give every asset a story.
3. **Design the party tray as a product.** Name it, give it a hero box and a printed menu card. This is the biggest open lane (white space 3).
4. **Treat packaging as the 2,000+ rating asset.** Branded bags and lids with a QR to leave reviews and order direct, and a "rice included" sticker.
5. **Lead with breadth.** A menu and signage hierarchy that shows North Indian, biryani and pulao, tandoor and Indo-Chinese side by side, against the specialists nearby.
6. **Ship a Diwali kit.** Social templates, a sweets-box sleeve and a tray sleeve, timed for the pitch, using only verified facts.
7. **Verify before pitching.** Scroll Kuppanna, Delhi6 and Roti Grill on Instagram and Google Maps by hand, so the "no one owns colour" claim is confirmed.
