# Chain Design Playbook: what big restaurant brands do, and what one Indian restaurant can copy

Prepared for the Curry District brand glow-up pitch. Everything below is described in my own words. No chain artwork or logos are reproduced.

**Method note and confidence labels.** The shared web-search budget (200 calls) ran out after about 20 of my queries, and most design-agency sites (Pentagram, Lippincott, Collins, JD&Co) are blocked from fetching. So the claims are only as deep as the search summaries I saw.
- **[H]** = stated in a search result I read this session (URL given).
- **[M]** = partly sourced, or consistent with sources but not directly confirmed.
- **[L]** = my background knowledge, unverified. Check before showing the owners.

Cost tiers (rough): **$** = under about $500 one-off, **$$** = about $500 to $3,000, **$$$** = over about $3,000.

## Chain by chain

**Chipotle**
- **Identity:** Hand-drawn, chunky, dark-brown lettering and doodles that make the packaging typographic and instantly recognisable. Design partner JD&Co has worked on it for 10+ years. Copy is light-hearted and ingredient-proud. [H] [Grits & Grids](https://gritsandgrids.com/2014/05/new-chipotle-brand-packaging-design/), [JD&Co](https://www.jdnco.com/case_study/chipotle-mexican-grill/)
- **Packaging:** The "Cultivating Thought" series printed short essays and stories (about 1,700 characters, a two-minute read) on cups and bags across 1,600 restaurants. A separate "Real Ingredients" run used illustrated lettering. [H] [Fast Company](https://www.fastcompany.com/3030737/now-you-can-read-jonathan-safran-foer-toni-morrison-michael-lewis-and-more-on-your-chipotle-). Lesson: it drew backlash for having no Mexican authors, so the content must reflect the culture it sits on. [H] [Fox](https://www.foxnews.com/lifestyle/chipotles-literary-packaging-faces-backlash-for-not-featuring-any-mexican-authors.amp)
- **Interior:** Open line, raw materials, minimal decoration. [L]
- **Digital:** Rewards launched in 2019 at 10 points per $1. An in-store QR scan earns points. Digital is about 37% of sales. A 2026 relaunch added monthly free-food drops and merch redemption. Digital-pickup "Chipotlane" stores out-sell the others by 10% or more. [H] [Hospitality Tech](https://hospitalitytech.com/chipotles-digital-sales-skyrocket), [Chipotle newsroom](https://newsroom.chipotle.com/2026-04-13-CHIPOTLE-RELAUNCHES-REWARDS-WITH-REWARDS-ON-REPEAT,-DELIVERING-MORE-VALUE-WITHOUT-TRADE-OFFS)
- **Steal:** Treat the bag as a reading surface that gives people a reason to keep it.

**Panda Express**
- **Packaging:** A box illustration that blends a Great Wall and a Golden Gate Bridge through a Chinatown archway, nodding to the classic American takeout-box look. The chain moved from styrofoam to paperboard, tested a paw-shaped three-compartment plate, and added compostable trays in 2025. [H] [QSR](https://www.qsrmagazine.com/news/panda-express-debuts-peking-pork-new-takeout-box/), [NRN](https://www.nrn.com/marketing-branding/panda-express-tests-new-container)
- **Steal:** One storytelling illustration on the takeout box that sums up the cuisine and the place.

**Cava**
- **Identity:** Sunny jonquil yellow with cream, tangerine and sky blues. The bold all-caps sans is based on Levantine sign painting. [H] [Mobbin](https://mobbin.com/colors/brand/cava), [Slant](https://blog.slantco.com/cava-logo-history-and-evolution/)
- **Packaging and serveware:** Custom dishware that is bright white and contoured inside to flatter the food, and matte-black with textured geometry outside. Polygraph handled digital, packaging and in-store. [H] [Polygraph](https://polygraphcreative.com/project/cava)
- **Steal:** A bowl or plate designed so the food is the colour and the vessel stays neutral. The brand pattern lives on the outside.

**Sweetgreen**
- **Identity:** Collins's refresh uses chartreuse on deep green, a custom type family inspired by Los Angeles reservoir-area signage, and a hand-drawn vintage-cookbook feel that celebrates imperfect ingredients. It covers store, signage, uniforms, packaging and app. [H] [NRN](https://www.nrn.com/fast-casual/sweetgreen-rebrands-with-new-logo-store-design), [Collins coverage](https://www.itsnicethat.com/news/collins-sweetgreen-graphic-design-080621)
- **Digital:** A redesigned app with illustrated elements. Its early loyalty (about $9 back per $99 spent) was too slow to feel, so rewards need a fast first win. [H] [NoGood](https://nogood.io/blog/sweetgreen-marketing-strategy/)
- **Steal:** Roll one system across uniform, bag and app so the brand feels inevitable.

**Shake Shack**
- **Identity:** Pentagram's system uses neon-style retro icons and a flexible kit that adapts to standalone sites, airports and stadiums. Packaging is simple. [H] [Pentagram](https://www.pentagram.com/work/shake-shack)
- **Interior:** Local artists paint murals at individual Shacks, often mixing neighbourhood landmarks with brand imagery. One Singapore artist was reported as the sixth local artist to work with the brand. [H] [Shake Shack blog](https://shakeshack.com/blog/local-news/the-art-of-shack-pop-artist-burton-morris-features-the-shack-logo-in-original), [Muros](https://www.muros.com/projects/shake-shack-downtown-brooklyn/)
- **Steal:** A fixed brand kit plus one locally painted wall.

**Chick-fil-A**
- **Identity:** The 1995 cows-with-misspelled-signs campaign (agency: The Richards Group). The cows are hand-drawn, minimal and cheeky, and they became the brand's character. Cow-print packaging and plush toys followed. [H] [Culinary Quests](https://culinaryquests.com/articles/chick-fil-a-cow-iconography-marketing/), [BrandVM](https://www.brandvm.com/post/chick-fil-a-marketing-strategy)
- **Digital and service:** An app and a famously polite service voice. [L]
- **Steal:** One lovable character with one running joke.

**Raising Cane's**
- **Interior:** Every restaurant is built around local memorabilia and art. The local crew works with the design team on what defines the community. 250+ disco balls trace back to the founder covering a skylight in the first store. Local artist murals and rhinestone feature walls appear at flagships. [H] [Cane's](https://www.raisingcanes.com/news/rooted-in-the-community/), [Tesselle](https://tesselle.com/blogs/design-inspiration/raising-canes-a-taste-of-entrepreneurship-community-and-art)
- **Steal:** One odd, ownable origin-story object repeated everywhere. For Curry District that could be a particular lamp, a brass cup or a hanging garland.

**Taco Bell**
- **Identity:** A Lippincott refresh (2016) with a simplified bell, a purple-to-magenta gradient, pop-art packaging inspired by the bell's sound, and added patterns. Frequent limited-time offers and app-only drops. The Defy drive-thru has three of four lanes reserved for mobile orders. [H] [Lippincott](https://www.lippincott.com/work/taco-bell-brand/), [Latterly](https://www.latterly.org/taco-bell-marketing-strategy/)
- **Steal:** Own a colour pair and a sound or gesture, and run rotating specials.

**Wingstop**
- **Identity:** Hunter green and gold with a vintage-aviation theme. The 1994 logo was only recoloured in 2023. A named flavour set (11 sauces) makes the menu itself the brand. [H] [Design Your Way](https://www.designyourway.net/blog/wingstop-logo/), [Logos World](https://logos-world.net/wingstop-logo/)
- **Steal:** Named, colour-coded flavours or heat levels.

**Starbucks**
- **Packaging:** A new seasonal cup every year since 1997. 2025 took cues from barista aprons (plaid, ribbon). Red Cup Day gives a free reusable holiday cup with a seasonal drink, and the 2025 cup carried a hand-drawn storefront illustration by an in-house designer. Interiors carry the same ribbon and garland motif. [H] [Starbucks](https://about.starbucks.com/stories/2025/its-holiday-cup-season-see-starbucks-new-festive-designs/), [Newsweek](https://www.newsweek.com/starbucks-red-cup-day-how-to-get-your-own-free-reusable-version-11038638)
- **Steal:** A seasonal packaging calendar and one "collectible" drop day.

**Dutch Bros**
- **Identity:** Blue and orange, a windmill emblem, "Broista" staff language, plaid and a Bigfoot motif, stickers with good-vibe slogans, and heavy merch. Quality of the sourcing is thin (shop listings). [M] [Etsy listings](https://www.etsy.com/market/dutch_bro_stickers), [Design Your Way](https://www.designyourway.net/blog/dutch-bros-logo/)
- **Steal:** Give staff a nickname and a slogan, and turn stickers into currency.

**Panera**
- **Digital:** A points-based MyPanera (10 points per $1) with a paid tier. Unlimited Sip Club offered about 30 self-serve drinks a month at $14.99, plus perks. [H] [NRN](https://www.nrn.com/fast-casual/panera-bread-rolls-out-new-and-improved-loyalty-program), [Panera](https://www.panerabread.com/en-us/press/press-room/panera-is-the-first-national-restaurant-company-to-offer-an-unlimited-self-serve-beverage-subscription.html)
- **Steal:** A small subscription for a high-frequency item. For Curry District that means chai or lunch.

**In-N-Out**
- **Identity:** Red, yellow and white, a logo unchanged since 1954, and a "secret menu" culture. [H] [1000logos](https://1000logos.net/in-n-out-burger-logo/)
- **Packaging and interior:** Tiny scripture references on wrappers and cups since 1987. Do not copy that (a religious device does not belong on an Indian restaurant's packaging; see Rule 10 on sacred symbols). Paper lap mats and tray liners tell the brand story. Red palm-tree wallpaper, schoolhouse lights and archive photos. A new collectible tee every year since 1984. [H] [Takeout](https://www.thetakeout.com/why-in-n-out-burger-prints-bible-verses-on-packaging-1849625516/), [NRN](https://www.nrn.com/quick-service/in-n-out-makes-decor-changes-at-20-restaurants), [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/In-N-Out_Burger_products)
- **Steal:** A hidden-surprise print on every wrapper and cup (non-religious: proverbs, spice trivia, chai wisdom), a placemat, and a yearly drop.

**Five Guys**
- **Identity and interior:** A trademarked red-and-white checkerboard, brown paper bags, open kitchen, no-frills design, and a box of free peanuts at the door. Checkerboard-themed clothing marked ten years in the UK. [H] [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Five_Guys), [Spot-On Branding](http://spot-onbranding.com/fiveguys/), [EconoTimes](https://econotimes.com/Five-Guys-Intensifies-Vneture-Into-Fast-Food-Fashion-1659011)
- **Steal:** One pattern as a pattern-owner, plus a free-snack waiting ritual.

**Domino's and Pizza Hut**
- **Domino's:** The 2008 pizza tracker made waiting visible, and it was refreshed with a lock-screen widget and AI time estimates. Illustrated box art. Boxes are mostly recycled. [H] [Fast Company](https://www.fastcompany.com/91519818/ai-has-come-for-dominos-pizza-tracker-and-were-not-mad-about-it), [Dieline](https://thedieline.com/dominos-pizza-box-illustrations/)
- **Pizza Hut:** A box that converts into a mini table, a round compostable box, and a box rated as a concert platform. [H] [Packaging Gateway](https://www.packaging-gateway.com/news/pizza-hut-packaging-box-table/), [Yanko](https://www.yankodesign.com/2019/10/22/pizza-huts-new-eco-friendly-round-boxes-are-engineered-to-make-the-pizza-tastier/)
- **Steal:** Make the wait or the box part of the fun, such as a status page or a lid that becomes a table tent.

**Regional heroes (all [L], background knowledge only)**
- **Whataburger:** An orange-and-white stripe system on a famous A-frame, plus a loud Texas fan culture.
- **Torchy's:** An irreverent devil mascot with a naughty copy tone.
- **Steal:** A regionally loved stripe or colour pair plus a fan-culture nickname.

## Principles that repeat across chains

1. **Design system over one-offs.** Pentagram, Collins and Lippincott deliver kits (colour, type, icons, patterns), not logos. Sweetgreen rolled its system across store, uniform, packaging and app.
2. **Own one colour or one pattern.** Cava has yellow, Wingstop green and gold, In-N-Out red and yellow, Five Guys the checkerboard, Taco Bell purple.
3. **A character or hand-drawn voice.** Chick-fil-A's cows, Chipotle's doodles, Dutch Bros' windmill.
4. **Packaging as billboard.** Cups, bags, lap mats and boxes carry stories (Chipotle essays, In-N-Out hidden prints, Panda's box art).
5. **Sensory brand moments.** Taco Bell's bell sound, Five Guys' peanuts, Cane's disco balls.
6. **QR to loyalty.** Chipotle scans an in-store QR for points. Sweetgreen's lesson: make the first reward arrive fast.
7. **Limited drops and merch.** Starbucks Red Cup Day, In-N-Out's annual tee, Taco Bell's rotating specials, Chipotle's merch exchange.
8. **Local-artist murals.** Shake Shack and Cane's both make each site a one-off.
9. **Seasonal packaging.** Starbucks resets its cup every year.
