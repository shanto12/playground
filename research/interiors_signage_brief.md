# Curry District: Interiors & Signage Brief (research, Oct 2026)

Scope: Suite 200, 11851 FM 423, Little Elm TX; assume 2,000-3,500 sq ft casual dining. Planning assumptions: 150 sq ft feature wall, about 15 tables, 5-10 booths, 60-100 sq ft of storefront glass. All prices are USD and approximate budgeting ranges, not quotes.

**Confidence tags.** **(S)** = figure taken from a web-search result summarised in this session (page text could not be opened; WebFetch was blocked). **(E)** = estimate from general industry knowledge, NOT verified by search. Treat every (E) as a placeholder until a quote arrives. The session's web-search quota ran out partway through, so tile, jaali, acoustics, plants, menus, patio, restroom and trend sections are (E) with no URLs.

## Key findings

**Wallpaper.** Type II commercial vinyl (about 20 oz per 54-inch linear yard, Class A / ASTM E84, scrubbable) is the restaurant standard. Stock rolls list at roughly $2-9 per sq ft; one listing shows $29.99 per linear yard on a 54-inch bolt, another $8.99 per sq ft (S). Spoonflower sells a Type II, Class A vinyl; its peel-and-stick is Type I paper and is marketed for short-term, low-traffic use even though it carries a Class A rating (S). Chasing Paper's peel-and-stick is described as approved for commercial uses such as restaurants (S). Verify the fire certificate before ordering. Photowall custom prints run about $3.65-6.50 per sq ft depending on finish, peel-and-stick at the top (S), but its fire rating and commercial suitability are unconfirmed. Dallas installation is quoted at $3-5 per sq ft for general jobs and $5.50-16 per sq ft for commercial or large-format work (S). A Class A, Type II product plus a pro installer is the lowest-risk route for an assembly occupancy. Ask the Little Elm fire marshal which interior-finish class applies (E).

**Murals.** Dallas hand-painted rates are reported at $22-30 per sq ft, with one DFW artist at about $12-15 per sq ft for large designs (S). Casa Ramen's 36-foot dining wall came in at $2,000-3,500 (S). Nationally, $10-50 per sq ft is typical and $35-100 for detailed work (S). Some muralists set project minimums of $5,000 or more (S). After-hours work for an operating restaurant adds about 50% to labour (S). Budget $100-1,000 for wall prep and sealant (S).

**Paint.** Benjamin Moore Scuff-X is rated best for scuff and washability in comparison pieces; Sherwin-Williams Emerald Urethane is strong on grease and kitchens; SW Scuff Tuff is aimed at high-wash corridors (S). Recommendation (E): satin or semi-gloss on wainscot and high-touch zones, matte-washable (low-sheen) above, flat for the ceiling. Dark saturated colours show scuffs and touch-up flashing more, so keep a labelled can for touch-ups. Commercial paint is about $60-100 per gallon (E).

**Neon and LED neon.** Custom LED neon: 24-30 in $180-500, 36-48 in $400-1,200, 60 in or larger $900-3,000+ (S). Typical per-foot pricing is $50-150 for simple designs and $150-300 for detailed or outdoor builds (S). Lead time is 2-4 weeks, with an express option of 10-14 business days (S, Yellowpop). Suppliers seen: Yellowpop, Vital Neon, Metrocenter Signworks (local-sign-shop option). An outdoor or window neon needs landlord approval and may need a permit.

**Backlit menu boards.** Light boxes with custom film inserts run about $100-200 each (S). Commercial digital menu displays run about $750-3,900 each (S).

**Window graphics.** Custom window decals start around $13 per sq ft, with solid vinyl as low as $4-8 and perforated film $6-15 (S). Installation on a typical storefront is $150-600 (S). Perforated film lasts roughly 1-3 years outdoors (S).

**A-frame signs.** Units cost about $98-249 and custom chalkboard versions $150-180 (S). Little Elm's sign code (Chapter 86) lists A-frame as a portable sign type; search results indicate a cap of 12 sq ft, 4 ft height, and one per tenant (S, partially confirmed; the code PDF is dated May 2013, so verify it is current).

**Little Elm sign permits.** The Town requires a sign permit from Building Safety before erecting, replacing, altering or even changing the face of a sign, except for items exempted in Table 86.1. The Building Official decides within 30 days of a complete application; no permit is issued for a property with outstanding sign violations (S). Wall-sign allowance is 3 sq ft per linear foot of primary building face, capped at 200 sq ft per building or tenant (S). Permitting phone is 214-975-0456 (S). The fee schedule document was not readable here, so the fee is unknown.

**Landlord approval.** Shopping-centre sign criteria such as Rayzor Ranch (Denton) require written landlord approval of all storefront and identification signage, with scaled drawings and shop drawings. The tenant pays for permits and fabrication, and a typical blade sign cap is 6 sq ft with 8 ft clearance (S). Curry District's own centre criteria were not found; ask the property manager for them first.

**Channel letters.** Front-lit letters run $3,000-7,000, halo-lit $4,000-9,000, and combination $5,000-12,000; $250-900 per letter (S). Re-skinning an existing sign face or tenant panel is far cheaper (E, about $300-1,200).

**Lighting.** Use 2700-3000K with CRI 90 or higher for dining. Hang pendants 28-34 in above the table and dim ambient light (S).

**Booths.** Reupholstery runs $200-600 per booth, average about $350, with performance fabric adding $50-100 (S). Reupholstery costs 40-60% less than replacement (S).

**Table tops.** Laminate is $10-20, solid wood $30-75 and stone or terrazzo $75-150 per sq ft (S). For a 30x30 in top that is roughly $60-125, $190-470 and $470-940 respectively.

## Design and concept notes

- **Photo corner.** Combine a mural or Type II wall, a small neon, a marigold garland arch and a bench. Include a subtle brand hashtag. Keep it free of deity imagery (project rule 10). Use pattern, colour and craft only.
- **Restroom surprise.** A bold washable Class A wallcovering, an arched mirror, and a witty line of copy. Keep ADA clearances and tactile signage intact (E).
- **Jaali and tile.** A jaali screen or a laser-cut partition can separate booth rows and break up sight lines. Check fire rating and sprinkler coverage for any panel material (E). Cement-look porcelain is the practical option for bar fronts or host stands.
- **Acoustics.** Fabric-wrapped or felt panels, ideally printed or patterned so they double as decor. Request a Class A rating (E).
- **Patio.** Whether the suite has a patio is unknown (open question). If so: string lights, planters with marigolds, cushions. Landlord, fire and any alcohol-service rules apply (E).
- **Menus.** Recommend a single-page cardstock or laminated menu with a QR tent for photos and the full menu. Avoid glossy lamination in photos (E). Never print prices unless research has marked them verified (Rule 5).
- **2026 trends (E, unverified here).** Maximalism, colour-drenching (walls, ceiling and trim in one hue), terrazzo, arches and rattan all align with Bazaar (loud, patterned) and Royal (arches, jali, brass). Chains use statement lighting, hero walls and open kitchens to prompt photos and lengthen dwell. Specific stats are unverified and should not be quoted.

## Phased plan

**$500 (DIY, nothing landlord-sensitive):** one accent wall or nook in washable paint (~$120 E), 10-12 faux marigold garlands (~$120 E), 2700K CRI 90+ LED bulb swap (~$80 E), chalkboard A-frame (~$110 S, check the 12 sq ft cap), QR cards (~$60 E). Total about $490.

**$2,500:** everything above, plus a 100 sq ft Type II photo wall with install (~$1,150 S), a 36 in custom LED neon (~$600 S), and window hours/logo decals (~$260 S). Total about $2,500.

**$10,000:** mural (120 sq ft at $22, ~$2,640 S), 5 booths reupholstered (~$1,750 S), 8 pendants plus electrician (~$1,300 E), perforated window film (~$800 S), restroom wallpaper (~$1,000 S), 48 in neon (~$900 S), A-frame, menus and QR (~$300 E), marigolds/plants (~$400 E), plus about $900 for permits and contingency. Total about $10,000. A channel-letter sign ($3,000-7,000 S) is a separate phase.
